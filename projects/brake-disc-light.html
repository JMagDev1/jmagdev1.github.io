Brake Disc Light

Using an old brake disc taken from my car and turned it into a working light.

  • 3D printed stand
  • Real Brake Disc

About This Project

I used an old brake disc fron my car and using an LED strip turned it into a multicolour light.

Key Features

  • Real Brake Disc
  • 3D printed stand
  • LED strip lights

Challenges & Solutions

One struggle i faced was getting the brake disc to sit upright without closing it in. Due to its centre of gravity it would tip forward.

What I Learned

By angling the brake disc at 5 degrees it allows it to sit sturdy and still be a statement piece without it being a safety concern.
