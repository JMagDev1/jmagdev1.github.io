Filing Cabinet

A filing cabinet built from 3/4" birch plywood, with soft-close runners, a black stain and a chrome handle.

F

About This Project

Built from 3/4" Birch Plywood to store all of my important documents. With a soft close drawer runner ensuring a premium feel. Couple that with a black stain and a chrome handle to match the rest of the furniture, suddenly a premium looking piece of furniture has been built.

Key Features

  • Soft-close runners
  • Large size

Challenges & Solutions

Ensuring a perfect 90 degree join for all of the corners, especially with basic equipment. Along with this, to keep the premium feel of the cabinet I needed to make sure there was an equal surround around all 4 sides of the drawer front. I used playing cards to achieve this.

What I Learned

An understanding of how to achieve a high-end finish on something that does not have to cost the earth. Sometimes a simpler solution can work just as well as a manufactured jig (playing cards).
